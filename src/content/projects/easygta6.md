GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026, and the first thing half the internet will search for is cheat codes. EasyGTA6 keeps it simple and trustworthy: a code goes on the site only after it has worked twice on a retail copy of the game.

## What it does

- **Button-by-button codes** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, laid out one button at a time so they're easy to follow.
- **Read aloud.** The site can read a code out one button at a time, so you can keep your eyes on the TV and your hands on the controller.
- **Keyboard shortcuts and a dark/light theme.**
- **A countdown to launch.** Until the game is out, the list is empty on purpose.

It's an unofficial fan site and isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
