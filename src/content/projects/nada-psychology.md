A website for a psychologist's private practice in Amsterdam. It introduces Nada, the areas she works on (trauma, anxiety, depression, addiction, identity and postpartum depression) and how to get in touch.

It's a single static page with no framework: hand-written HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. It has proper search and link-preview metadata, so it shows up well in Google and when the link is shared.
