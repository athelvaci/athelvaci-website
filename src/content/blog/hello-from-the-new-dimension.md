My old site was a Gatsby starter from 2020. It did its job, but it looked like a place where I had parked a resume. This time I wanted somewhere that feels like me: a bit nerdy, a bit playful, and easy to keep up to date.

## What's new

- A portal that does something. Click it and the whole site hops to another dimension: mint, sunset or night.
- A periodic table of the tools I use, instead of a bullet list.
- A map of my career, newest job first, so scrolling down is traveling back in time.
- Blog posts and projects are plain Markdown files, so adding one takes minutes.

## What to expect here

I'll write about what I run into at work and on the side: Java and Spring Boot, Vue.js, PostgreSQL, Azure, the odd migration story, and whatever sci-fi or techno rabbit hole I fell into that week.

Adding a post is just a file with a little front matter at the top:

```md
---
title: My next post
description: One line that shows up in the list.
date: 2026-11-10
tags: [java]
---

Write the post here.
```

If something looks broken, email me.
